Telegram Ad Platform — Official Specifications & Policies

Sponsored messages on Telegram are designed to respect user privacy. Ads are targeted strictly based on the context of public channels, bots, or search queries where they appear—never using personal user profiling or behavioral tracking.

1. Ad Format & Character Limits

  • Maximum text length: 160 characters (including spaces).
  • Line breaks and bulleted lists inside ad copy are prohibited.
  • Destination URL must point to a public Telegram channel, post, or bot (t.me/...).

2. Auction & CPM Pricing

  • Minimum CPM starts at 0.10 TON (or 2.00 EUR for Euro accounts).
  • Higher CPM bids win priority placement in high-traffic public channels.
  • Unused campaign budget can be reclaimed at any time by stopping the ad.

3. Prohibited Content & Formatting

  • Excessive capitalization (ALL CAPS) or clickbait claims are automatically rejected.
  • Unlicensed financial guarantees, shock content, and misleading buttons are prohibited.

4. Moderation Turnaround

  • New campaigns enter In Review immediately upon submission.
  • Standard moderation completes within 15–60 minutes.
